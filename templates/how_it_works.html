The Grievance Redressal Lifecycle

An 8-stage transparent journey from citizen submission to final closure.

Official 8-Stage Procedure
1
Citizen Submits Grievance

Citizen fills the online grievance form, specifying district, category, description, and optional supporting photos/documents. Gemini AI analyzes the text to recommend department and priority.

2
System Generates Unique Reference ID

The portal generates a unique tracking code (e.g., KL-2026-PWD-8401) stored securely in MySQL. Status is set to Submitted, and an initial history log entry is created.

3
CMO Reviews Grievance

Chief Minister's Office administrative officers examine the complaint, verify authenticity, review AI-generated summaries, and determine priority (Low, Medium, or High).

4
CMO Assigns Department & Officer

The grievance is routed to the competent government department (e.g. PWD, Health, KSEB). Status transitions to Under Review, and the assignment is permanently logged in MySQL.

5
Department Officer Investigates

The assigned department officer accesses the complaint from their isolated departmental dashboard, begins ground inspection, adds field notes, and marks status as In Progress.

6
Officer Submits Action Taken Report (ATR)

Upon completing physical works or administrative remedies, the officer compiles a comprehensive Action Taken Report, uploads verification photos, and submits it to the CMO.

7
CMO Reviews and Approves Resolution

The CMO administrator audits the department's ATR. If satisfactory, the administrator marks the grievance as Resolved. If inadequate, the complaint is escalated or reopened.

8
Citizen Receives Updates & Feedback

The citizen tracks the resolution on the portal, reads the officer's report, and provides a 1-to-5 star rating with comments. If unsatisfied, the citizen can dispute for reopening.

The Five Grievance Statuses Explained
Status Actor Responsible Definition
Submitted Citizen Grievance successfully lodged online. Awaiting initial administrative screening by CMO.
Under Review CMO Admin CMO has reviewed the complaint, assigned it to the appropriate department, and issued directives.
In Progress Department Officer Field investigation or remedial engineering/administrative work is actively underway.
Resolved CMO Admin Action Taken Report verified and approved by CMO. The grievance is formally closed.
Reopened CMO Admin / Citizen Resolution contested by citizen or flagged for reinvestigation due to recurring ground issues.
