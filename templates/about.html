ABOUT THE INITIATIVE

Chief Minister's Public Grievance Redressal Portal

Empowering citizens through transparent, accountable, and technology-driven governance.

Purpose & Background

The Chief Minister's Public Grievance Redressal Portal is conceived as a comprehensive single-window grievance platform. In traditional administrative setups, citizens often navigate fragmented departmental offices without clear visibility into complaint status, responsible officials, or resolution timeframes.

This portal directly connects every citizen with the Chief Minister's Office (CMO), bridging geographical and bureaucratic barriers. By harnessing modern web technologies and AI-assisted triage, the portal establishes verifiable accountability, structured workflows, and real-time status tracking for public problems across Kerala.

Our Vision

To realize a citizen-centric, transparent, and technology-enabled governance model where every public grievance receives prompt, impartial, and measurable administrative redressal, fostering trust between the people and their government.

Our Mission

To streamline grievance intake, eliminate unnecessary delays, enforce strict departmental accountability, leverage artificial intelligence for intelligent categorization, and provide citizens complete visibility throughout the redressal lifecycle.

Core Objectives
Single-Window Access

One centralized portal for submitting issues across roads, water, health, education, electricity, and local amenities.

Time-Bound Redressal

Establish priority assessment, proactive overdue tracking, and automated escalation to prevent unattended complaints.

AI-Assisted Processing

Integrate Google Gemini LLM to suggest departments, assess urgency, summarize issues, and flag duplicate concerns.

Citizen Feedback Loop

Allow citizens to rate resolution quality and reopen complaints when the ground reality remains unresolved.
