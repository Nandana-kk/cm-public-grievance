EASY 4-STEP PROCESS

How Grievance Redressal Works

A clear and accountable pathway from citizen submission to final resolution.

1
Submit Online

Citizen provides complaint details and uploads supporting proof. Built-in Gemini AI assists in categorizing.

2
CMO Review

Chief Minister's Office evaluates priority and assigns the grievance to the competent department officer.

3
Field Action

Designated department officer inspects, undertakes corrective action, and uploads an Action Taken Report (ATR).

4
Redressal & Feedback

CMO verifies the report, closes the grievance, and the citizen receives updates with an option to provide rating.
