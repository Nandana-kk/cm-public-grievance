Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Common queries regarding grievance submission, reference numbers, tracking, and escalation.

To submit a grievance, you must first register for a citizen account or sign in with your email and password. Click on "Submit Grievance" in the navigation bar or dashboard, select your Kerala district and grievance category, provide a detailed description of the problem, optionally upload photos or supporting documents, and click Submit.

Every successfully submitted complaint generates a unique reference number formatted as KL-2026-XXX-XXXX (for example: KL-2026-PWD-8401). This number is your permanent tracking identifier. You can enter this reference number on the "Track Grievance" page without logging in to see the real-time status and historical timeline of administrative action.

The portal features an integrated AI Assistant powered by the official Google Gemini API (model gemini-2.5-flash). When you enter your grievance description, the AI analyzes the text, suggests the most appropriate Kerala government department and category, assesses the urgency level (Low, Medium, or High), and produces an executive summary for the CMO review team. You can click "Apply Suggestions" to auto-fill the form.

The portal uses exactly five official statuses:
  • Submitted: Grievance has been recorded and is awaiting administrative screening.
  • Under Review: CMO has examined the complaint and assigned it to the appropriate department.
  • In Progress: Department officer has initiated field inspection or remedial execution.
  • Resolved: Action Taken Report (ATR) has been verified and approved by the CMO.
  • Reopened: Case reopened due to citizen feedback or ground verification issues.
